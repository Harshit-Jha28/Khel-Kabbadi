Version: 0.1

Engine: Phaser 3.90
Language: TypeScript
Platform: Android (Primary), Windows (Development)

---

# Vision

Khel-Kabbadi is not just a Kabaddi game.

It is a complete Kabaddi career simulator where every player has a career, personality, rivals, media attention, contracts, injuries, and ambitions.

The goal is to create the Football Manager of Kabaddi while keeping the on-court gameplay fun and skill-based.

## 3. Living World

The world never stops.

Even when the player is not playing:

• Transfers happen
• Players retire
• New players appear
• Teams change coaches
• Injuries happen
• Records are broken
• Rivals improve
• League tables update

---

## 4. Dynamic Storytelling

No scripted career.

Every save creates a unique story.

Examples:

• Rivals
• Media controversy
• Fan reactions
• Contract disputes
• Injury setbacks
• Legendary comeback

No two careers should feel identical.
